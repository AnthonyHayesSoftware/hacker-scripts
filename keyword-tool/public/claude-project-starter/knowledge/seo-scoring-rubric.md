# Traditional SEO scoring rubric (/100)

Score each article against these criteria. Show the per-section points, the
total, and the top 3 fixes to raise the score.

## 1. Search intent match — 20 pts
- Content type matches the dominant intent (informational / commercial /
  transactional / navigational). (8)
- Covers the subtopics a satisfied searcher expects. (7)
- No intent mismatch (e.g. a sales page for an informational query). (5)

## 2. Keyword & topic coverage — 15 pts
- Primary keyword in H1/title, first 100 words, and naturally throughout. (5)
- Cluster/secondary keywords mapped to relevant sections. (5)
- Related entities and synonyms present (no over-optimisation). (5)

## 3. Title & metadata — 10 pts
- Title ≤60 chars, compelling, contains primary keyword. (4)
- Meta description ≤155 chars, accurate, with a reason to click. (3)
- URL slug short and descriptive. (3)

## 4. Structure & readability — 15 pts
- Logical H2/H3 hierarchy; one H1. (5)
- Short paragraphs, lists/tables where helpful, scannable. (5)
- Reading level appropriate to the audience. (5)

## 5. Depth & E-E-A-T — 15 pts
- Demonstrates experience/expertise; specific, accurate, current. (6)
- Author/brand signals and sources where relevant. (5)
- Comprehensive vs. competing pages, without padding. (4)

## 6. Snippet & SERP-feature readiness — 10 pts
- Direct answers positioned for featured snippets. (5)
- FAQ/how-to/table formatting where the SERP rewards it. (5)

## 7. Internal linking & CTA — 10 pts
- Relevant internal links with descriptive anchors. (5)
- Clear next step / CTA matching the funnel stage. (5)

## 8. Technical hygiene (content-side) — 5 pts
- Descriptive image alt text, no orphan headings, sensible length. (5)
